Brand

Clear. Accessible. Productive.

Built on the IBM Carbon Design System (open source, Apache 2.0): Carbon's White and g100 themes, Blue 60 for actions, IBM Plex Sans on Carbon's type scale, square corners, tiles, fields with a bottom border, pill tags and inline notifications. Plain-English content patterns follow GOV.UK's style guidance. Everything here is driven by tokens in assets/css/styles.css.

Typography

IBM Plex Sans on Carbon's type scale: Light 300 for display and large figures, Regular 400 for headings and text, SemiBold 600 for emphasis and small headings. Labels are 12px with 0.32px tracking.

Principles

  • Plain English first. Spell out every acronym once per page.
  • Never colour alone. Every coloured signal also has a text label.
  • Sources visible. Every deadline shows when it was checked and links to the official source.
  • Sponsors look different. Gold "Sponsored" label, separate from editorial content.
  • Accessible: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard access, visible focus rings, reduced motion respected.
